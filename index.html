PMCH Google Docs review

A local tool maintained by Peter Harrison for reading and reviewing academic manuscripts in Google Docs, available to Peter and a limited group of Cambridge Centre for Music and Science collaborators.

Each operator signs in with their own Google account and authorizes the shared application on their own computer. We recommend a dedicated review account for each operator. Document owners choose which documents to share with that account. The operator then uses their local agent to read a requested document and, when authorized, submit native suggestions and anchored comments.

The tool requests Google Docs access for documents available to the review account. It also requests per-file Google Drive access to verify the signed-in account. Access remains subject to each document's sharing permissions.

Sharing the application registration does not share operators' Google accounts, local tokens or document permissions. Operators do not need to register a separate application. The Desktop client configuration is supplied privately; account tokens and manuscript content are not published here.

The local agent may process document content through its model provider, such as OpenAI when using Codex. Each operator should check their chosen provider's applicable terms and account settings. See the privacy information for processing, storage and removal.

Support: pmcharrisonbot@gmail.com. This contact address is not the review account that every operator should use.
